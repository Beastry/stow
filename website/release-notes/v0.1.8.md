## Stow 0.1.8

This update adds optional quick extraction from Finder, media playback in hover previews, and more control over split archives and batch extraction.

- Turn on Double-click in Finder extracts to unpack archives beside their originals. Hold Shift as the archive opens to browse it instead. The setting is off by default; disk images and installer packages always open for browsing.
- Apply your extraction preferences to Finder and Services requests, including same-name folder prompts and shared answers for a batch.
- Fix an exFAT extraction failure in installed release builds.
- Play audio and video inside hover previews.
- Split ZIP and 7Z archives into a requested number of parts.
- Collect a batch's extracted results into a new folder.
- View and copy a ZIP archive's comment in Archive Info, keeping long comments out of the file listing.
- Open the running version's release page from Help > Release Notes.
- Keep windows where you left them and open new windows more predictably.
- Refresh the app icon, packing settings, Tray destination controls, and batch password sheet.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
