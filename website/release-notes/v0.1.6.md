## Stow 0.1.6

This update restores authored DMG installation windows, adds support for Zstd-compressed 7Z archives, and improves extraction to external drives.

- Open supported DMG installers with their original background, app icons, icon positions and Applications destination in Browser and Glance. Install by dragging the app onto its destination or choosing Install App, with the existing permission and signature checks.
- Correctly recognize disk images whose volume names contain version numbers. Missing or unsupported layout information falls back to ordinary browsing.
- Show apps' own icons throughout archive browsing, and provide a control for showing or hiding disk-image layout files in ordinary views.
- Keep keyboard selection limited to the files displayed on the installer canvas, and preserve background alignment when the canvas expands.
- Read and extract 7Z archives compressed with Zstd, including encrypted and split archives, with bounded decoding and error handling.
- Improve extraction, installation and Tray export to exFAT and other destinations that require copying files into place. Preserve conflicts, cancellation recovery and Mac metadata, and correct exFAT free-space reporting.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
