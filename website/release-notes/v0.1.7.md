## Stow 0.1.7

This update improves compatibility with real-world DMG installers, including OpenWhispr, Steam, and Kimi.

- Read larger Finder layout records and older installer layouts, preserving the image's background, icon positions, and authored sizing.
- Recognize background images stored at the volume root and Finder Applications aliases, keeping installation helpers out of Extract All.
- Load declared app icons that use a named ICNS companion.
- Preserve safe internal relative links during extraction so application bundles keep their original signatures. Links that escape the extracted content remain blocked.
- Preserve installer destinations when renaming or rereading an archive, and refresh them when its contents change.

Requires macOS 26 or later.
