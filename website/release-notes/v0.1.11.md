## Stow 0.1.11

- Choose **Each in Its Own Archive** when packing multiple files or folders. You can cancel or retry individual items and reveal each finished archive in Finder.
- Extract ZIPs whose files use different passwords. Skip a locked item to continue, then review what was extracted and what was left out. Stow keeps the original archive if extraction is incomplete.
- Saving passwords in Keychain and automatically unlocking archives are separate settings. You can explicitly reuse a saved password when packing.
- Choose how to handle name conflicts when adding files to an archive. Stow also warns before saving changes to a signed JAR because those changes can invalidate its signature.
- Switch between list and icon views in Glance. Drag a selection to Finder, or expand archive folders directly in Quick Look.
- Browser history restores your search. Hold Shift when opening an archive in Finder to reverse your usual open-or-extract choice for that operation.
- Small ZIP and 7z archives that fit within the requested part size are saved as ordinary archives. This update also fixes superseded Quick Look listings and keeps simultaneous archive operations from clearing each other’s temporary files.

Requires macOS 26 or later.

## 简体中文

- 一次打包多个文件或文件夹时，可以为每个项目单独创建压缩包。每个项目都可以单独取消或重试，打包完成后可在访达中查看。
- ZIP 里的文件用了不同的密码，也能逐个解压。遇到打不开的项目，可以先跳过，完成后再查看哪些文件已解压、哪些还没解压。只要有项目没解压，Stow 就会保留原压缩包。
- 在钥匙串中保存密码、自动解锁压缩包，现在可以分别设置。打包时也可以手动选择使用已保存的密码。
- 向压缩包添加文件时，可以选择如何处理同名项目。保存已签名 JAR 的改动前，Stow 会提醒你签名可能失效。
- 速览可以切换列表和图标视图，也能把选中的项目拖到访达。在快速查看中，可以直接展开压缩包里的文件夹。
- 浏览历史会恢复当时的搜索。在访达打开归档时按住 Shift，可以临时切换「打开归档」和「直接解压」。
- ZIP 或 7z 如果不超过分卷大小，会保存成一个普通压缩包。这次也修复了切换归档后快速查看仍在等待旧列表的问题，并确保同时进行的归档操作不会清理彼此的临时文件。

需要 macOS 26 或更新版本。
